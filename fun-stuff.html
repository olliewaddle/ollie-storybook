Draw Along! ✏️

How to Draw a Duckling:

  1. Draw a big circle for the body (like a fluffy ball!)
  2. Draw a smaller circle on top for the head
  3. Add a little orange triangle for the beak
  4. Draw two tiny dots for eyes
  5. Add little wing shapes on the sides
  6. Draw two tiny feet at the bottom
  7. Color it YELLOW! 🐤

Don't worry if it looks wobbly - Ollie's towers are crooked too and that's okay!
