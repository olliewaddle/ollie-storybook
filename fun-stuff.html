How to Draw a Duckling:

1

Draw a big circle for the body!

2

Add a smaller circle on top for the head!

3

Add an orange triangle beak!

4

Draw a little dot for the eye!

5

Add a little wing on the side!

6

Draw two little feet!

7

Color it YELLOW! 🐤 YOU DID IT!
