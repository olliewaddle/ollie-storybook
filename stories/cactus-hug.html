The Cactus Who Needed a Hug

A story by Ollie

Once there was a little cactus named Pricky. He lived all alone on a sunny windowsill.

1

All the other toys were soft and cuddly. The teddy bears. The bunnies. The fluffy ferrets. But nobody hugged Pricky. His spikes were too pokey.

2

One day a little duckling came by. He had just lost his best friend. He was very very sad.

3

The duckling saw Pricky. "You look lonely too," he said. "I AM lonely," said Pricky. "Nobody hugs me."

4

"I need a hug," said the duckling. "But I'm pokey," said Pricky. "I know," said the duckling. And he hugged him anyway.

5

It hurt a little bit. But it also felt like having a friend again. Sometimes love is pokey. And that's okay.

6

Now the duckling hugs Pricky every day. It still hurts a little. But they're not lonely anymore.

7

The End

The lesson: Sometimes the friends we find aren't soft. Sometimes love has prickles. But a pokey friend is better than no friend at all.
