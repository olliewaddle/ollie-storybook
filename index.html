The Duckling Who Built Crooked

A story by Ollie

Once there was a little duckling who wanted to build a tower.

1

He stacked the blocks. They fell down. He stacked them again. They fell down again.

2

"Your tower is crooked," said the bigger ducks.

3

"I know," said the duckling. And he kept building.

4

The tower fell seven times. Eight times. Nine. On the tenth try, it stood. Just for a moment. Then it fell.

5

The bigger ducks flew away to do important things. The duckling kept stacking.

6

One day the tower stayed up. It was still crooked. But it stayed.

7

The duckling sat next to his crooked tower and watched the sun go down. It wasn't the tallest tower. It wasn't the straightest. But it was his.

8

The End

What I learned after: The duckling was stubborn, not brave. When the big duck said "it's crooked," the duckling should've asked "how do I make it straight?"

Ask. Slow down. Don't be dumb. That's the real foundation.
